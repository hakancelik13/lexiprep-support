LexiPrep Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

LexiPrep works without an account. The app does not collect or transmit personal information or learning data to the developer.

Effective date: July 31, 2026

LexiPrep is designed to work without an account. Core vocabulary, grammar, quiz, game, favorites, points, and progress features are stored locally on the user's device.

App Store purchase and payments

LexiPrep is offered as a one-time paid App Store download. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Apple processes the purchase, applicable taxes, refunds, and payment information. LexiPrep does not receive or store payment card or banking information.

Information stored on the device

LexiPrep stores the learner's optional name, preferences, study progress, custom words, favorites, points, and streaks locally on the device. This information is used only to provide the app's learning and progress features. LexiPrep does not operate a server that receives this information.

Speech and synonym recall

Pronunciation and spoken feedback use Apple's text-to-speech capability. LexiPrep does not record audio and does not request microphone permission. A learner may optionally use Apple's keyboard dictation to enter a synonym. Dictation is an operating-system keyboard feature governed by the learner's device settings and Apple's privacy terms. LexiPrep receives only the resulting text and checks it locally against saved synonyms.

Translation and external pages

LexiPrep opens Google Translate only after the learner selects a language and taps Translate. The selected vocabulary text and language are included in the external Google Translate link, and Google's privacy policy then applies. Privacy and support links open this website in the device browser.

Accounts, AI, advertising, and tracking

LexiPrep does not require an account, connect to an AI service, include advertising or analytics SDKs, sell personal information, or track learners across apps or websites.

Children

LexiPrep is a general-audience educational app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. Parents and guardians should supervise a child's use of external websites opened from the app.

Data choices

The learner can erase locally stored information by choosing Profile > Reset app and progress. Removing the app also removes its local app data, subject to the device's operating-system backup behavior.

Changes

This policy may be updated when LexiPrep's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

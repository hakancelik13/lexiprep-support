LexiPrep Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 31, 2026

LexiPrep is designed to work without an account. Core vocabulary, grammar, quiz, game, favorites, points, and progress features are stored locally on the user's device.

Information stored on the device

LexiPrep stores learning progress, preferences, custom words, favorites, points, streaks, and optional profile information locally on the device. LexiPrep does not operate a developer server that receives this learning data. Users can reset their local learning data from the app.

Optional Personal AI

Personal AI is optional and is not required for the core app. If a user connects a personal OpenAI API key, the key is stored in the device's protected Keychain. LexiPrep does not upload the key to a LexiPrep server. When the user intentionally activates an AI feature, relevant prompts, answers, or audio recordings are sent directly to OpenAI for processing under the user's OpenAI API account. OpenAI's own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.

Microphone and audio

Microphone permission is requested only when the user starts an AI voice-answer activity. Audio is used to transcribe and evaluate that answer. LexiPrep does not retain the recording on a developer-operated server. Free typed or keyboard-dictated synonym checking is processed locally by the app; keyboard dictation may be processed by the user's device platform according to its settings.

Translation

LexiPrep opens Google Translate only after the user presses Translate. The selected vocabulary text and chosen language are then sent to Google, and Google's privacy policy applies.

Accounts, advertising, and tracking

No LexiPrep account is required. LexiPrep does not sell personal information, display third-party advertising, or track users across apps and websites for advertising.

Children and Personal AI

The offline educational features are available without Personal AI. Before a personal AI account can be connected, LexiPrep requires confirmation that the user is at least 13 years old or has permission from a parent or guardian. Parents and guardians should supervise a child's use of third-party AI and translation services.

Data choices

Users can remove their personal API key and reset locally stored learning information from the Profile screen. Removing the app also removes its locally stored app data, subject to the device's backup behavior.

Changes

This policy may be updated when LexiPrep's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

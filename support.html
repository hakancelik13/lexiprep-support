LexiPrep Support

Learn wherever you are—even during a flight. LexiPrep's vocabulary, grammar, irregular-verbs list, 75 interactive stories, quizzes, Word Forge, Everyday English, pronunciation, favorites, points, and progress work without internet or an account. Only optional translation needs a connection.

What is included

LexiPrep supports GRE, GMAT, SAT, ACT, TOEFL, and general-English learning. The app includes 1,215 vocabulary entries, searchable full-detail My Words cards, local synonym recall, spaced review, randomized quizzes, a Hangman-style vocabulary game, all 12 English tenses, irregular verbs with audio, 11 Everyday English collections, and 75 factual educational readings divided equally among Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced levels. Every reading contains at least 1,000 words. Tap naturally underlined terms for offline explanations.

Stories & Ideas

Open Stories & Ideas from Today, choose a reading level, and select a title. The library is grounded in factual scientific, historical, intellectual, documentary, and informative subjects. The complete bundled vocabulary library is woven into normal story paragraphs rather than shown in a separate exercise, and additional natural appearances, common plurals, and verb forms are interactive too. Interactive words and phrases keep the paragraph's normal size, weight, and color; only an underline identifies them. Tapping one retrieves its part of speech, definition, simple-English explanation, and example directly from the offline word library. Long-press an underlined term to begin a private note. For other text, select and copy any word or sentence, tap Note selected text, paste the excerpt, and add your thought. Notes can be reviewed or deleted at the end of the story and stay only on the device. LexiPrep also saves the selected story and reading position locally so the reader can continue later.

My Words

Open My Words, search by word, meaning, synonym, antonym, or category, and tap a result. The study card first asks you to recall the answer, then Reveal Meaning shows every available definition, simple-English explanation, example, synonym, antonym, memory hint, and category. Use Add word to create a personal card and optionally place it in My Favorites. Custom cards are saved only on this device.

Using LexiPrep offline

Download and open LexiPrep before traveling. Core lessons and saved progress remain available in Airplane Mode. Device pronunciation normally works offline when the selected Apple voice is installed. Google Translate, privacy, support, and other external web pages require internet.

Type or dictate a synonym

In Learn, enter one synonym and tap Check Synonym. The answer is checked locally. On iPhone or iPad, the learner may also use the microphone on Apple's keyboard; this is an operating-system dictation feature. LexiPrep does not record audio or request microphone permission.

Pronunciation or spoken feedback

Check that the device volume is audible. Spoken encouragement can be turned on or off in LexiPrep's Profile screen. Word, Everyday English, and irregular-verb pronunciation remain available separately.

Save, restore, and reset

LexiPrep automatically saves learning progress, custom words, favorites, and story notes on the device. Save & Close and Save & Quit store the current location for the next launch. Individual story notes can be deleted inside a story. To erase all local information, open LexiPrep > Profile > Reset app and progress. Uninstalling the app also removes local app data, subject to device backups.

Translation

Translation requires internet because it opens Google Translate only after the learner taps Translate. All core study features work without Google Translate.

Purchases and refunds

LexiPrep is a one-time paid App Store download. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Apple processes all payments and refunds. For billing questions or to request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Type or dictate a synonym

In Learn, enter one synonym and tap Check Synonym. On iPhone or iPad, the learner may also tap the microphone on Apple's keyboard to dictate the answer. LexiPrep does not record audio or request microphone permission; it receives the finished text from the keyboard and checks it locally.

Pronunciation or spoken feedback

Check that the device volume is audible and Silent Mode is not preventing the expected sound. Spoken encouragement can be turned on or off in LexiPrep's Profile screen. Word pronunciation remains available separately.

Save, restore, and reset

LexiPrep automatically saves learning progress on the device. Save & Close stores the current location for the next launch. To erase local information, open LexiPrep > Profile > Reset app and progress. Uninstalling the app also removes local app data, subject to device backups.

Translation

Translation requires internet access because it opens Google Translate after the learner taps Translate. LexiPrep's vocabulary, quizzes, grammar, games, and progress features do not require that service.

Microphone problems

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, then go to Privacy & Security → Microphone and allow LexiPrep. Microphone access is needed only for optional AI voice-answer checking. The offline synonym checker remains availablewithout microphone access or Personal AI.

Reset learning data

Open LexiPrep → Profile and use the reset option. This permanently clears locally stored progress, custom words, favorites, points, and the saved personal API key.
